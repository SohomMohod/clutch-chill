# clutch-chill

A zero electricity palm cooler built for people who sweat a lot ( palmar hyperhidrosis) or people working hours who cannot afford sweat like digital artists or engineers.

### Why I am building this
Places where room temperature easily exceeds 35 degree celsius, like where I live , 15 minutes of gaming of tedious work like sketching and soldering can cause your palm to sweat a lot and I myself suffer from this a lot, so I want to build a passive cooling system that doesn't require electricity and that's why I thought of clutchChill.

### How ClutchChill works 
ClutchChill uses no electricity and cools your palm using special material called PCM (Phase Changing Material). When the user puts there palm on the top aluminum plate. A 2mm air gap made by springs that acts as an insulator closes under the hands weight and the PCM comes in contact with it. Since aluminum is a great conductor of heat, the heat from the user's palm will be absorbed by the PCM. When the hand is removed from the top plate, the springs push it upwards and create a ~2mm air gap which prevents heat loss by the top plate. This contraction will be 100% electricity less and to recharge the material, you just swap the melted PCM with a recharged one( you put it inside a fridge to recharge.)
